### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**What is KML?**

KML (Keyhole Markup Language) is an XML-based format used to represent geographic data in Google Earth and other mapping applications. It's often used for displaying features like points, lines, polygons, and 3D models on a map.

**Why Convert KML to JPEG?**

Converting KML files to JPEGs allows you to easily share your geographic data with others who may not have access to Google Earth or other mapping software. It's also useful for creating presentations, reports, and websites that incorporate maps.

**Is the Conversion Accurate?**

Our converter strives to produce accurate representations of your KML data in JPEG format. However, complex KML files with intricate features or 3D models may not be perfectly replicated due to limitations in the conversion process.

**Can I Convert Other File Formats?**

Currently, our tool only supports KML to JPEG conversions. We are constantly working on adding support for other file formats in the future.
