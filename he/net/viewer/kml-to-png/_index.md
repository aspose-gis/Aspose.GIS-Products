### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is a KML file?**
A: KML (Keyhole Markup Language) is an XML-based format used to represent geographic data, including points, lines, polygons, and more. It's commonly used for displaying maps in Google Earth and other mapping applications.

**Q: What is PNG?**
A: PNG (Portable Network Graphics) is a raster graphics file format that supports lossless compression. It’s widely used for images on the web due to its good image quality and support for transparency.
