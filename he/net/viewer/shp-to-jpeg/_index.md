## SHP to JPEG Viewer

This viewer allows you to convert shapefiles (.shp) into JPEGs.

### Features

*   Convert individual or multiple shapefiles.
*   Control JPEG quality and resolution.
*   Preview the resulting JPEG before downloading.
*   Simple and intuitive interface.

### Usage

1.  Upload your SHP file(s).
2.  Adjust the quality and resolution settings.
3.  Click "Convert" to generate the JPEG image.
4.  Download the converted JPEG file.

### Configuration Options

*   **Quality:** Adjust the JPEG compression level (0-100, higher is better quality but larger file size).
*   **Resolution:** Set the desired resolution of the output JPEG image.
*   **Scale:** Scale factor for the shapefile data.

### Troubleshooting

*   If you encounter any issues, please check that your SHP file is valid and not corrupted.
*   Ensure that you have sufficient disk space to store the converted JPEGs.
*   Contact support if the problem persists.
