## Overview

This tool converts OpenStreetMap data to PNG images. It allows you to specify the area of interest, zoom level, and other parameters to generate customized map tiles.

## Features

*   Convert OSM data to PNG images
*   Specify area of interest (bounding box)
*   Adjust zoom level for desired detail
*   Customize output image size
*   Support various output formats (e.g., PNG, JPEG)
*   Easy-to-use web interface

## Usage

1.  Enter the latitude and longitude coordinates for the top-left and bottom-right corners of your area of interest.
2.  Select a zoom level. Higher zoom levels provide more detail but may result in larger image sizes.
3.  Specify the desired output image size (width and height).
4.  Click the "Generate" button to create the PNG image.

## Parameters

*   **Top-Left Latitude:** The latitude coordinate of the top-left corner of the area of interest.
*   **Top-Left Longitude:** The longitude coordinate of the top-left corner of the area of interest.
*   **Bottom-Right Latitude:** The latitude coordinate of the bottom-right corner of the area of interest.
*   **Bottom-Right Longitude:** The longitude coordinate of the bottom-right corner of the area of interest.
*   **Zoom Level:**  Determines the level of detail in the output image. Higher values mean more detail.
*   **Width:** The width of the output PNG image in pixels.
*   **Height:** The height of the output PNG image in pixels.

## Example

To generate a PNG image of San Francisco, California:

*   Top-Left Latitude: 37.7000
*   Top-Left Longitude: -122.5000
*   Bottom-Right Latitude: 37.8000
*   Bottom-Right Longitude: -122.4000
*   Zoom Level: 12
*   Width: 512
*   Height: 512

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter any issues, please check the following:

*   Ensure that the latitude and longitude coordinates are valid.
*   Verify that the zoom level is within the supported range.
*   Make sure that the output image size is reasonable.
*   Check your internet connection.
