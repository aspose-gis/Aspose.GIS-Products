## Overview ##

The OpenStreetMap (OSM) viewer allows you to explore and interact with OpenStreetMap data directly within our platform. This provides a powerful tool for visualizing geographic information, performing spatial analysis, and integrating OSM data into your workflows.

## Features ##

*   **Interactive Map:** Pan, zoom, and tilt the map to view different areas of interest.
*   **Data Layers:** Toggle various OSM data layers on and off, such as roads, buildings, landuse, and points of interest.
*   **Search Functionality:** Quickly locate specific addresses or places by name.
*   **Customizable Basemaps:** Choose from a variety of basemap styles to suit your preferences.
*   **Data Querying:** Retrieve attribute information for features on the map.
*   **Export Options:** Export map images and data in various formats.

## Usage ##

1.  Navigate to the OpenStreetMap Viewer within the platform.
2.  Use the controls to pan, zoom, and tilt the map.
3.  Select data layers to display specific types of information.
4.  Utilize the search bar to find locations of interest.
5.  Explore feature attributes by clicking on elements on the map.

## Technical Details ##

The OpenStreetMap Viewer is built using [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com/) and utilizes the [OpenStreetMap API](https://www.openstreetmap.org/api). Data is rendered as vector tiles for efficient loading and rendering.

### Performance Considerations ###

*   **Zoom Level:** Higher zoom levels display more detailed data, which can impact performance.
*   **Data Layers:** Displaying too many data layers simultaneously can slow down the viewer.
*   **Network Connectivity:** A stable internet connection is required to load map tiles and data.
