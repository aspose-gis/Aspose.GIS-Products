## Converting GeoTIFF to JPEG

This guide explains how to convert GeoTIFF files to JPEG format using our online viewer.  The process is straightforward and requires no special software installation.

### Steps

1.  **Upload Your GeoTIFF:** Drag and drop your `.tif` file onto the designated area or click "Choose File" to select it from your computer.
2.  **Adjust Conversion Settings (Optional):** You can modify parameters like JPEG quality, image size, and color correction to fine-tune the output.
3.  **Start Conversion:** Click the "Convert" button to begin the conversion process.
4.  **Download Your JPEG:** Once the conversion is complete, a download link will appear. Click it to save the resulting `.jpg` file to your device.

### Supported GeoTIFF Features

Our converter supports various GeoTIFF features including:

*   Georeferencing
*   Multiple Bands
*   Compression (LZW, JPEG)
*   Metadata

### Troubleshooting

*   **File Size Limits:**  Large files may take longer to convert or exceed our upload limits. Consider splitting large GeoTIFFs into smaller tiles.
*   **Unsupported Formats:** Ensure your file is a valid GeoTIFF (`.tif`). Other raster formats are not supported.
*   **Conversion Errors:** If you encounter errors, try reducing the image size or adjusting JPEG quality settings.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

*   **Is this service free?** Yes, our GeoTIFF to JPEG conversion is completely free.
*   **What's the maximum file size allowed?** The maximum file size is 50MB.
*   **Can I convert multiple files at once?** Currently, we only support converting one file at a time.
