# Convert to MGRS

The Military Grid Reference System (MGRS) is a grid system used by military organizations around the world. It allows for the identification of locations on Earth using a combination of letters and numbers.

## How to convert coordinates to MGRS

There are several ways to convert geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) to MGRS:

*   **Online converters:** Many websites offer free online tools for converting coordinates to MGRS.
*   **GIS software:** Most GIS software packages, such as QGIS and ArcGIS, have built-in functionality for converting coordinates to MGRS.
*   **Programming libraries:** Several programming libraries are available for converting coordinates to MGRS in various languages.

## Example

For example, the coordinates 34.0522° N, 118.2437° W convert to the MGRS grid square 18QNU 69783.
