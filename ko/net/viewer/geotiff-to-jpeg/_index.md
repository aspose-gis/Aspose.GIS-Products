## Converting GeoTIFF to JPEG

This guide explains how to convert a GeoTIFF file into a standard JPEG image format using our online viewer. This process allows you to easily share and display your geospatial data without requiring specialized software.

### Steps for Conversion

1.  **Upload Your GeoTIFF:** Begin by uploading the GeoTIFF file you wish to convert. Our viewer supports various GeoTIFF formats and sizes.
2.  **Adjust Parameters (Optional):** You can adjust parameters like image resolution, color correction, and contrast to optimize the output JPEG for your specific needs. These options are available in the viewer's settings panel.
3.  **Initiate Conversion:** Once you’re satisfied with the preview, click the "Convert to JPEG" button to start the conversion process.
4.  **Download Your JPEG:** After the conversion is complete, a download link will appear. Click this link to save the resulting JPEG image to your device.

### Supported GeoTIFF Features

Our viewer supports common GeoTIFF features including:

*   Georeferencing information
*   Multiple bands (e.g., RGB, multispectral)
*   Compression schemes (e.g., LZW, Deflate)
*   Metadata embedded within the file

### Troubleshooting

*   **File Size Limits:**  Large GeoTIFF files may take longer to upload and convert. If you encounter issues with very large files, consider splitting them into smaller tiles or contacting support.
*   **Unsupported Features:** While we support a wide range of GeoTIFF features, some less common formats might not be fully compatible. In such cases, please contact us for assistance.
*   **Conversion Errors:** If you encounter any errors during the conversion process, double-check your file and try again.  If the problem persists, provide details about the error message and your GeoTIFF file to our support team.
